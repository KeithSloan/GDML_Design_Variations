## Study

Alternating absorber and active layers, 200 x 200 mm, starting at z = 0,
inside a vacuum envelope.  Every absorber and active layer is a sensitive
detector (copy number = layer), so the analysis has the energy deposited in
each, per event.  The variants change the absorber, the active medium, the
layer thicknesses and the number of layers, at roughly constant depth in
radiation lengths for electrons.

**Objective:** Best electromagnetic energy resolution (sigma/E of the active-layer signal for 1 GeV electrons) for a compact depth, and an e/pi signal ratio close to 1; note where the physics lists disagree for pions.

**Primary metric:** `resolution` (lower is better)
